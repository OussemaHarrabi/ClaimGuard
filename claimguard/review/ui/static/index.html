ClaimGuard AI — reviewer interface

The deterministic 15-rule check results and the reviewer's decisions, side by side. Synthetic claims only.

A PASS is not payer approval. The pack's rulebook states it plainly:

PASS means this specific check passed on the supplied data. It does not mean payer approval or a clinically correct claim.

(fictional-rulebook, docs/04_Rulebook.md:16.) No claim is submitted to a payer from this page, and NOT_IMPLEMENTED is never shown as a pass.

Reviewer identity is self-declared. This page has no login, no session and no permission model: whoever types a name into the reviewer field is recorded as the actor of the decision. Do not put personal data in it.

Instructional oracle, not ground truth. The claims, codes and policy are invented for teaching. This interface is not clinical or reimbursement ground truth, and it reports no real-world denial reduction. Every status shown here came from the deterministic engine; every decision is recorded by the API in an append-only ledger.
