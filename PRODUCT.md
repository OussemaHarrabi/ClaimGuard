# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ClaimGuard serves RCM and medical-billing administrators who validate claim readiness before submission. They work through queues for long sessions, often under time pressure, and need to understand why a claim is blocked without reading raw rule output. Review leads and compliance owners use the same surface to supervise decisions, trace evidence, and confirm that corrections were made consistently.

## Product Purpose

ClaimGuard turns deterministic claim checks into an operator-ready review workflow. It must help an administrator find the next important claim, understand the evidence behind every finding, apply or request the right correction, recheck the claim, and leave an auditable decision trail. Success means fewer preventable submission errors, faster review, and explanations that remain faithful to the deterministic engine.

## Brand Personality

Familiar, trustworthy, empowering. The product should feel immediately legible to healthcare-administration professionals familiar with Velodoc, while presenting ClaimGuard as a focused pre-submission assurance tool. Its voice is calm, concise, specific, and never theatrical.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI dashboards that lead with chat instead of the operator's queue.
- Marketing-style gradients, oversized hero metrics, and decorative glass effects inside operational workflows.
- Dense legacy hospital software that hides critical actions behind small controls and unexplained codes.
- A literal Velodoc clone or any use of another company's private assets, copy, or proprietary identity.
- Autonomous-agent theatre that implies the model can adjudicate, submit, or alter a deterministic claim outcome.

## Design Principles

1. **Earn familiarity.** Reuse the interaction grammar and calm blue-aqua atmosphere jurors already understand, then make ClaimGuard's evidence workflow unmistakably its own.
2. **Evidence before explanation.** Show the deterministic finding, cited evidence, and correction contract before optional AI-generated prose.
3. **Keep the administrator in control.** Every suggested correction is reviewable, reversible, attributable, and separated from terminal decisions.
4. **Design for the queue.** Optimize scanning, filtering, keyboard movement, and progressive disclosure for an all-day desktop workflow.
5. **Make trust inspectable.** Surface provenance, rule versions, recheck history, confidence boundaries, and audit state without forcing users into raw JSON.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All critical meaning must survive without color, every workflow must be keyboard operable, focus must remain obvious, and touch targets must be usable on smaller devices. Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain readable contrast in every status state, support zoom and text expansion, and avoid hover-only disclosure. The initial interface is English, but layouts and components must tolerate longer localized text.
